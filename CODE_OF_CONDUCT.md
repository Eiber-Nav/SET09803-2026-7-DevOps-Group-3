Code of Conduct
[!NOTE]
This Code of Conduct outlines the standards expected from all members contributing to this project. As a Scrum team, we aim to maintain a respectful, collaborative, and productive working environment throughout the development of the population reporting system.
1. Respect All Team Members
   •	Treat every team member with respect and professionalism.
   •	Be considerate of different opinions, ideas, backgrounds, and levels of experience.
   •	Provide constructive feedback and avoid personal criticism.
   •	Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.
2. Communicate Effectively
   •	Communicate clearly and respectfully with other members of the team.
   •	Keep team members informed about progress, problems, and anything that may affect assigned tasks.
   •	Participate in Scrum meetings and project discussions where possible.
   •	Ask for assistance when needed and provide help to other team members when possible.
3. Collaborate as a Scrum Team
   •	Work together to achieve the goals and requirements of the project.
   •	Participate appropriately in Sprint Planning, Sprint Reviews, Retrospectives, and other agreed Scrum activities.
   •	Keep assigned tasks and their progress up to date throughout each Sprint.
   •	Respect the priorities agreed upon by the team and recorded in the project backlog.
   •	Share relevant knowledge, ideas, and resources with the team.
   •	Give appropriate credit to team members for their contributions.
   •	Resolve disagreements through constructive discussion and focus on what is best for the project.
4. Maintain Quality Contributions
   •	Ensure code and documentation are clear, understandable, and relevant to the project requirements.
   •	Test changes where appropriate before they are added to the main project.
   •	Follow agreed coding standards, naming conventions, and repository structure.
   •	Avoid making unnecessary changes to another team member's work without first communicating with them.
   •	Review contributions carefully to reduce errors and maintain the overall quality of the system.
5. Use Git and GitHub Responsibly
   •	Write clear and meaningful commit messages that describe the changes made.
   •	Use branches and pull requests according to the workflow agreed upon by the team.
   •	Avoid committing incomplete, unnecessary, or unrelated files to the repository.
   •	Review changes before merging them into the main branch.
   •	Never intentionally delete, overwrite, or modify another team member's work without discussion.
   •	Never commit passwords, database credentials, API keys, or other sensitive information to the repository.
6. Be Accountable
   •	Complete assigned tasks within the agreed timeframe whenever possible.
   •	Inform the team as early as possible if a task cannot be completed on time.
   •	Take responsibility for mistakes and work with the team to correct them.
   •	Contribute fairly to the workload and overall success of the project.
7. Follow Ethical and Academic Standards
   •	Ensure that all submitted work follows the academic integrity requirements of the course.
   •	Do not knowingly submit copied or plagiarized work as an original contribution.
   •	Acknowledge external resources, libraries, documentation, or other material where appropriate.
   •	Ensure that contributions can be clearly identified through appropriate GitHub commits and project documentation.
   •	Use project data and resources only for their intended purpose.
8. Address Problems Appropriately
   •	Raise concerns about project work or team interactions respectfully and as early as possible.
   •	Attempt to resolve minor disagreements through constructive discussion within the team.
   •	Serious or unresolved issues should be raised with the appropriate project supervisor or module teaching staff.
   •	Concerns should be handled fairly, professionally, and respectfully.
   [!IMPORTANT]
   By contributing to this project, all team members agree to follow this Code of Conduct and work together in a professional and respectful manner.
   The success of the project depends on effective communication, collaboration, accountability, and the contribution of every team member.
   All members are encouraged to support one another and contribute towards creating a reliable and high-quality population reporting system.
